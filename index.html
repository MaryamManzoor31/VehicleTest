💡 AUTOMOTIVE ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES GUIDE

⚡ Power-to-Weight Ratio

  • • Acceleration is governed by $F = m \cdot a$.
  • • Reducing 100 kg often yields faster lap times than adding 50 HP.
  • • Lighter vehicles brake later and corner with higher lateral Gs.

🌀 Forced Induction (Boost)

  • • Turbochargers utilize hot waste exhaust gasses to compress air.
  • • Superchargers are belt-driven for instant zero-lag response.
  • • Trade-off: Higher cylinder pressures require high-octane fuel & cooling.

🛞 Tire Traction & Contact Patch

  • • Racing slicks generate maximum friction ($\mu > 1.0$) when warm.
  • • Wider tires provide superior cornering but increase rolling resistance.
  • • Wet conditions require tread sipes to evacuate water and avoid hydroplaning.

🔩 Suspension Dynamics

  • • Lowering ride height reduces body roll and lowers center of gravity.
  • • Excessively stiff dampers cause loss of traction over uneven bumpy tarmac.
  • • High-downforce wings provide high-speed stability at the cost of top speed drag.
